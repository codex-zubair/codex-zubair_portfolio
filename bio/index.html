The short version

I'm Zubair Alam — an AI Software Engineer from Bogura, Bangladesh. I design and build end-to-end products: the AI layer, the backend, the app, and the store listing. My work spans AI app development, Android / Kotlin & Flutter, full-stack web platforms, VPN clients and autonomous systems.

On Fiverr I'm a top-rated Level 2 seller with 40+ completed orders and a 5.0★ record. Alongside client work, I contribute to an Air Force research & development project and maintain a large open-source footprint across AI, radio/SDR tooling, computer vision and embedded systems.

The longer version

I started out in Bogura, completed my schooling at Bogura TTC, then moved to Dhaka for college at Daffodil College. I went on to earn my BSc in Computer Science & Engineering from the European University of Bangladesh, where I built my foundations in algorithms, systems, AI and software engineering.

Today I work as a software engineer in industry and simultaneously contribute to an Air Force R&D project — applied engineering in simulation, signal processing and autonomous systems. In parallel I ship client products through Ryza Dev, my studio, taking ideas from blank repo to signed release.

I'm currently working toward a master's abroad in a technologically advanced country — deepening my engineering and AI research skills. Until then, if it's an AI product that needs to go from idea to the store, that's the work I do.

My motto: Ship it. Make it work. Then make it better.
