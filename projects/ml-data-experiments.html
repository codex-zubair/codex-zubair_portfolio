Overview

A body of applied machine-learning work spanning regression, classification, NLP sentiment analysis, voice age prediction and reinforcement-learning experiments.

Selected experiments

  • Regression & classification — supervised models with feature engineering, cross-validation and error analysis.
  • NLP sentiment analysis — text preprocessing, embeddings and classifier evaluation.
  • Voice age prediction — audio feature extraction and regression on voice characteristics.
  • Computer vision — detection and tracking experiments that later fed into autonomous-systems work.
  • Reinforcement learning — agents trained in simulated environments to explore control strategies.

Why it matters

These experiments are the foundation for the production AI work: they build the intuition for what models can and can't do, how to evaluate them honestly, and how to move from a notebook to something that runs reliably in an app.
