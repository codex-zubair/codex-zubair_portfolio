Overview

Contributing engineering to a national defense research and development project spanning simulation, signal processing and autonomous systems.

The work

The project applied simulation and signal-processing techniques to autonomous systems problems. Due to the sensitive nature of the work, specific implementation details are not published here, but the areas of contribution include:

  • Simulation of dynamic systems and evaluation of control strategies.
  • Signal processing and radio tooling, including SDR-based experimentation.
  • Autonomous-system behaviours — sensing, path planning and control.
  • Applied research support and technical documentation.

Relevance to my client work

This R&D work sharpened the same skills I bring to product engineering: rigorous simulation before shipping, signal and data processing, and autonomy that behaves predictably under uncertainty. It's the research backbone behind the computer-vision and autonomous-systems work in my client projects.
