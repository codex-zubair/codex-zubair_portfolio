Zerova / Zystra VPN

Cross-platform VPN client·Shipped to Google Play

RoleMobile engineering, publishing
StackFlutter · Dart · Kotlin · WireGuard
PlatformsAndroid (Google Play)
TypeProduct · Security · Rebrand

Overview

A cross-platform VPN client built on a WireGuard core, with native tunnel management, multi-region server selection, and a complete privacy and rebrand lifecycle — designed, built, signed and shipped to Google Play.

The problem

The client needed a real VPN: not a wrapper around someone else's SDK, but a proper tunnel with native service integration, reliable connection state, and store-compliant privacy handling. It also had to survive a full rebrand from Zerova to Zystra without breaking existing installs or the signing chain.

Approach

  • Built the tunnel in the native Android layer via VpnService and the WireGuard core, keeping the Flutter UI as a thin state reflector.
  • Implemented connection lifecycle — connect, disconnect, reconnect-on-network-change and a foreground service notification.
  • Added multi-region server selection with latency-aware defaults and per-region configs.
  • Wrote the VPN service declaration, data-safety form and privacy policy to match actual app behaviour.
  • Preserved the signing key through the rebrand so Play treated the new identity as an update.

Result

A production VPN client delivered through the full store lifecycle — including VPN declaration, data-safety compliance and a rebrand handled without losing update continuity on Google Play.
